## Slide 1 - Title

Position the analyst role as a business partner role, not a reporting queue.

## Slide 2 - Why the Analyst Role Changed

Modern teams need judgment, not only tool execution.

## Slide 3 - Request Taker vs Business Partner

Compare the behaviors that make the same technical skill look junior or senior.

## Slide 4 - The Decision Lens

Move every request toward a decision, owner, deadline, and action.

## Slide 5 - Business Context Checklist

Use a simple intake structure before touching data.

## Slide 6 - Stakeholder Mapping

Understand decision maker, user, blocker, and subject matter expert roles.

## Slide 7 - Prioritization and Pushback

Trade scope, time, certainty, and polish without damaging trust.

## Slide 8 - Quality and Ownership

Clarify what must be checked before a number leaves your hands.

## Slide 9 - Real World Example

Transform a vague revenue question into an actionable investigation plan.

## Slide 10 - Self-Assessment

Reflect on work intake, ownership, communication, and reliability.

## Slide 11 - Take-Home

Apply the intake template to one real request from work or a portfolio project.
