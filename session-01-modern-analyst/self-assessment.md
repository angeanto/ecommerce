# Self-Assessment - Session 1 - Modern Data Analyst: From Request Taker to Business Partner

## Google Forms Setup

Form title: Data Career Accelerator - Self-Assessment - Session 1

Description: Use this assessment to reflect on your readiness after Session 1. The goal is not to score perfectly. The goal is to identify which behaviors and skills need deliberate practice.

Scale:

- 1 - Rarely true
- 2 - Sometimes true
- 3 - Mostly true
- 4 - Strongly true
- 5 - Consistently true

## Section - Work Intake
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I clarify the decision before starting analysis.
- I ask who will act on the result.
- I define scope, deadline, and output format early.
- I can explain when a request is actually several questions.

## Section - Business Understanding
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I connect metrics to business processes.
- I ask what a stakeholder currently believes.
- I separate symptoms from possible causes.
- I can name the trade-off behind a requested analysis.

## Section - Ownership and Reliability
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I check definitions before sharing numbers.
- I flag uncertainty clearly.
- I provide useful progress updates.
- I take responsibility for the usefulness of the final output.

## Section - Communication
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I summarize findings in business language.
- I adapt detail level to the audience.
- I can push back without sounding unhelpful.
- I end analysis with a recommendation or next step.


## Scenario Check

A stakeholder asks for a dashboard by Friday, but cannot explain the decision it will support. What do you ask before accepting the work, and what trade-off do you propose?

Suggested response format:

- What is the real business problem?
- What information do you need next?
- What action would you recommend?
- What risk or assumption should be communicated?

## Reflection

- What is one skill from this session that already feels strong?
- What is one behavior you will practice this week?
- What evidence would show that you improved?
