# Self-Assessment - Session 5 - Business Thinking: Domains, KPIs, Stakeholders, and Strategy

## Google Forms Setup

Form title: Data Career Accelerator - Self-Assessment - Session 5

Description: Use this assessment to reflect on your readiness after Session 5. The goal is not to score perfectly. The goal is to identify which behaviors and skills need deliberate practice.

Scale:

- 1 - Rarely true
- 2 - Sometimes true
- 3 - Mostly true
- 4 - Strongly true
- 5 - Consistently true

## Section - Business Context
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I can identify the business process behind a metric.
- I ask why a metric matters before analyzing it.
- I can explain the commercial impact of a data issue.
- I understand that the same metric can mean different things by domain.

## Section - KPI Thinking
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I can build a KPI tree from a strategic objective.
- I separate outcome metrics from driver metrics.
- I include guardrail metrics in recommendations.
- I avoid over-optimizing one metric without context.

## Section - Stakeholders
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I can map decision makers, users, blockers, and experts.
- I consider stakeholder incentives before presenting findings.
- I can anticipate where metric definitions may create disagreement.
- I make trade-offs explicit in recommendations.

## Section - Strategic Communication
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I connect findings to business options.
- I explain uncertainty and risk clearly.
- I recommend actions that match the decision owner's control.
- I can say what not to do based on the data.


## Scenario Check

Growth wants to promote the highest-revenue shops. Finance worries about refunds and failed payments. How do you redesign the analysis so both concerns are represented?

Suggested response format:

- What is the real business problem?
- What information do you need next?
- What action would you recommend?
- What risk or assumption should be communicated?

## Reflection

- What is one skill from this session that already feels strong?
- What is one behavior you will practice this week?
- What evidence would show that you improved?
