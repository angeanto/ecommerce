# Session 8 - Career Positioning, CV, and Job Strategy

## Position in the Program

Participants translate their course work into a sharper professional narrative, stronger CV, proof-of-work portfolio, interview stories, and focused job strategy.

## Core Questions

- Which role family and business problems do I want to be known for?
- How do I prove impact rather than list tools?
- What weekly system will create career momentum?

## Session Goals

- Define a clear target role thesis.
- Rewrite CV bullets using impact, scope, and business value.
- Use course artifacts as proof-of-work assets.
- Build interview stories around ambiguity, trade-offs, and outcomes.
- Create a 90-day job or internal progression plan.

## Files in This Folder

- `presentation.md` - Version-controlled slide outline and speaker notes for the Canva deck.
- `examples.md` - E-commerce database examples, code, and facilitation notes.
- `self-assessment.md` - Google Forms-ready self-assessment blueprint.
- `take-home-assets.md` - Homework, templates, and participant deliverables.

## Live Session Flow

1. Framing and business relevance.
2. Core teaching concepts.
3. E-commerce database walkthrough or work sample.
4. Participant reflection and debrief.
5. Homework setup.

## Expected Participant Output

By the end of this session, participants should have a concrete artifact they can reuse at work or in their portfolio, not only notes from the lesson.
