## Example 3 - Interview story seed

Story topic: Fixing a revenue metric disagreement.

- Context: Finance and Growth had different revenue definitions.
- Problem: One counted captured payments, the other counted order totals.
- Action: Mapped source tables, defined grain, created a metric contract, and added dbt tests.
- Result: Stakeholders agreed on a governed reporting model.
- Reflection: A definition problem can look like a data quality problem until ownership is clarified.
