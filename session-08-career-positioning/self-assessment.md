# Self-Assessment - Session 8 - Career Positioning, CV, and Job Strategy

## Google Forms Setup

Form title: Data Career Accelerator - Self-Assessment - Session 8

Description: Use this assessment to reflect on your readiness after Session 8. The goal is not to score perfectly. The goal is to identify which behaviors and skills need deliberate practice.

Scale:

- 1 - Rarely true
- 2 - Sometimes true
- 3 - Mostly true
- 4 - Strongly true
- 5 - Consistently true

## Section - Positioning
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I can name the role family I am targeting.
- I can explain the business problems I want to solve.
- I can connect my technical skills to domain value.
- I can say no to roles that do not match my thesis.

## Section - CV and LinkedIn
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- My CV bullets emphasize impact over tasks.
- My profile has a clear headline and value summary.
- My tools are presented as supporting evidence.
- My selected projects prove the role I am targeting.

## Section - Interview Readiness
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I have stories for ambiguity, conflict, data quality, and impact.
- I can explain trade-offs I considered.
- I can discuss what I would improve next time.
- I can answer technical questions using business context.

## Section - Execution
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I have a weekly application and outreach cadence.
- I track conversion rates and feedback.
- I improve one weak market signal each week.
- I have a 90-day plan for momentum.


## Scenario Check

Your CV lists many tools but gets few interviews. How do you rewrite your positioning and proof so it signals business impact?

Suggested response format:

- What is the real business problem?
- What information do you need next?
- What action would you recommend?
- What risk or assumption should be communicated?

## Reflection

- What is one skill from this session that already feels strong?
- What is one behavior you will practice this week?
- What evidence would show that you improved?
