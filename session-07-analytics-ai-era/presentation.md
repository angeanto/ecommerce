## Slide 1 - Title

Position AI as leverage, not a replacement for analytical judgment.

## Slide 2 - What Changes

Explain which tasks become faster and which responsibilities become more important.

## Slide 3 - AI Workflow Map

Use AI for brainstorming, SQL drafts, documentation, QA, and communication.

## Slide 4 - Context Prompting

Give schema, grain, business question, constraints, and desired output.

## Slide 5 - Verification

Check row counts, grain, filters, joins, and definitions before trusting output.

## Slide 6 - Future Skills

Develop domain fluency, problem framing, data modeling, and judgment.

## Slide 7 - Governance and Privacy

Avoid exposing sensitive data and document AI-assisted work.

## Slide 8 - Common Failure Modes

Spot hallucinated columns, double counting, and unsupported claims.

## Slide 9 - Real World Example

Review an AI-generated SQL draft for revenue analysis.

## Slide 10 - Self-Assessment

Reflect on AI readiness and verification discipline.

## Slide 11 - Take-Home

Create a reusable prompt and verification checklist.
