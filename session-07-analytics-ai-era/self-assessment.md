# Self-Assessment - Session 7 - Analytics in the AI Era

## Google Forms Setup

Form title: Data Career Accelerator - Self-Assessment - Session 7

Description: Use this assessment to reflect on your readiness after Session 7. The goal is not to score perfectly. The goal is to identify which behaviors and skills need deliberate practice.

Scale:

- 1 - Rarely true
- 2 - Sometimes true
- 3 - Mostly true
- 4 - Strongly true
- 5 - Consistently true

## Section - AI Use Cases
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I can identify analytics tasks where AI is useful.
- I know which tasks still require human judgment.
- I can use AI to accelerate documentation and communication.
- I avoid using AI output as final without review.

## Section - Prompting
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I provide schema context when asking for SQL.
- I include business definitions and filters.
- I specify the desired output and audience.
- I ask AI to explain assumptions and risks.

## Section - Verification
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I check grain before trusting generated SQL.
- I verify columns and joins against the schema.
- I test outputs with row counts and small samples.
- I can identify overconfident or unsupported explanations.

## Section - Governance
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I protect sensitive data in AI-assisted workflows.
- I document when AI helped create an artifact.
- I know when not to use AI for a task.
- I keep accountability for the final analytical output.


## Scenario Check

AI gives you a confident SQL answer for an executive dashboard metric. What do you check before sending it to stakeholders?

Suggested response format:

- What is the real business problem?
- What information do you need next?
- What action would you recommend?
- What risk or assumption should be communicated?

## Reflection

- What is one skill from this session that already feels strong?
- What is one behavior you will practice this week?
- What evidence would show that you improved?
