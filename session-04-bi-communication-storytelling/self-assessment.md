# Self-Assessment - Session 4 - BI, Communication, and Data Storytelling

## Google Forms Setup

Form title: Data Career Accelerator - Self-Assessment - Session 4

Description: Use this assessment to reflect on your readiness after Session 4. The goal is not to score perfectly. The goal is to identify which behaviors and skills need deliberate practice.

Scale:

- 1 - Rarely true
- 2 - Sometimes true
- 3 - Mostly true
- 4 - Strongly true
- 5 - Consistently true

## Section - Dashboard Design
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I define the user and decision before choosing charts.
- I can separate monitoring metrics from diagnostic metrics.
- I avoid putting every available metric on one page.
- I make definitions visible enough for users to trust the view.

## Section - Storytelling
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I lead with the message, not the chart type.
- I explain implication and recommended action.
- I use context and comparison before declaring a trend.
- I can write a concise executive summary.

## Section - Visual Judgment
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I choose chart types based on the comparison needed.
- I avoid decoration that competes with the data.
- I use consistent labeling and metric formatting.
- I know when a table is better than a chart.

## Section - Stakeholder Usefulness
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I know who owns each dashboard action.
- I design for the review cadence of the audience.
- I collect feedback after launch.
- I remove or revise unused dashboard elements.


## Scenario Check

An executive dashboard has 30 charts and no one uses it. How would you diagnose the issue and redesign the first page?

Suggested response format:

- What is the real business problem?
- What information do you need next?
- What action would you recommend?
- What risk or assumption should be communicated?

## Reflection

- What is one skill from this session that already feels strong?
- What is one behavior you will practice this week?
- What evidence would show that you improved?
