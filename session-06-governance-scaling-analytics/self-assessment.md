# Self-Assessment - Session 6 - Data Governance and Scaling Analytics

## Google Forms Setup

Form title: Data Career Accelerator - Self-Assessment - Session 6

Description: Use this assessment to reflect on your readiness after Session 6. The goal is not to score perfectly. The goal is to identify which behaviors and skills need deliberate practice.

Scale:

- 1 - Rarely true
- 2 - Sometimes true
- 3 - Mostly true
- 4 - Strongly true
- 5 - Consistently true

## Section - Ownership
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I can name the owner of a core metric.
- I know who approves definition changes.
- I can separate technical owner from business owner.
- I document ownership where users can find it.

## Section - Definitions
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I write metric definitions in business language.
- I define grain, filters, exclusions, and refresh cadence.
- I include examples and non-examples for ambiguous metrics.
- I know when two metrics should not share the same name.

## Section - Quality
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I use tests to protect critical models.
- I reconcile important metrics against source totals.
- I monitor freshness for time-sensitive data.
- I communicate data incidents clearly.

## Section - Scaling
Use a linear scale from 1 to 5.

- I promote reusable models instead of copying logic.
- I review dashboards after launch.
- I retire unused assets.
- I think about access and privacy before sharing data.


## Scenario Check

Finance and Growth use two different revenue numbers in leadership meetings. What governance process would you run to resolve and prevent the issue?

Suggested response format:

- What is the real business problem?
- What information do you need next?
- What action would you recommend?
- What risk or assumption should be communicated?

## Reflection

- What is one skill from this session that already feels strong?
- What is one behavior you will practice this week?
