## Slide 1 - Title

Make governance practical rather than bureaucratic.

## Slide 2 - Governance Mindset

Explain governance as trust, ownership, and repeatability.

## Slide 3 - Metric Ownership

Define who owns logic, sign-off, usage, and change control.

## Slide 4 - Definitions and Glossary

Create clear business definitions for metrics and dimensions.

## Slide 5 - Data Quality

Use tests, reconciliation, freshness, and anomaly checks.

## Slide 6 - Access and Privacy

Respect data sensitivity and least-privilege access.

## Slide 7 - Scaling Analytics

Promote reusable models and retire unused assets.

## Slide 8 - Dashboard Lifecycle

Plan launch, feedback, review, and deprecation.

## Slide 9 - Real World Example

Resolve a revenue disagreement between Finance and Growth.

## Slide 10 - Self-Assessment

Reflect on ownership, quality, and scale readiness.

## Slide 11 - Take-Home

Create a metric contract and dashboard lifecycle plan.
