# Take-Home Assets - Session 6 - Data Governance and Scaling Analytics

## Homework

- Write a metric contract for captured revenue.
- Add a governance checklist to one dashboard concept.
- Identify one dbt test that would prevent a business-facing error.

## Reusable Assets

- Metric contract template.
- Data quality checklist.
- Dashboard lifecycle review template.

## Submission Guidance

Participants should submit a concise artifact rather than a long narrative. A good submission should include:

- The business question or role target.
- The relevant metric, model, dashboard, or communication artifact.
- The assumptions and trade-offs.
- The next action the artifact supports.

## Review Rubric

- Clarity: The artifact can be understood without a live explanation.
- Business relevance: The artifact connects to a decision, stakeholder, or role outcome.
- Technical reliability: The artifact includes enough checks, definitions, or assumptions to be trusted.
- Practicality: The artifact can be reused at work, in a portfolio, or in interview preparation.
